# Case Study on Fiscal Policy Impact on Textile Industry
### BCom 1st Year - Economics Project
**Student:** Vishwa Poojitha | **Industry:** Tiruppur Textile Industry

### 1. Introduction
Fiscal Policy is government's use of taxation and public spending to influence economy. In India, it is announced via Union Budget.

### 2. Company/Industry Profile
Industry: Tiruppur Textile - Known as Knitwear Capital of India
Turnover: Rs 60,000 Crore (2024), 60% export to Europe & US

### 3. Case Situation
In 2024, Tiruppur industry faced crisis: Cotton price increased 30%, GST increased from 5% to 12%, Export orders dropped by 15%.

### 4. Relevant Macroeconomic Policy
Union Budget 2024-25 Fiscal Policy: Govt announced PLI Scheme for Textiles (Rs 10,683 Crore) and removed 11% import duty on cotton.

### 5. Application of Policy
Govt applied by: 1. Giving 5% interest subsidy for textile loans 2. Cotton import duty 0% from Jan 2025

### 6. Analysis of Impact
After policy, raw material cost decreased by 20%. Export recovered.
- 2023 Export: 35,000 Cr
- 2024 Export: 28,000 Cr (down)
- 2025 Expected: 40,000 Cr (up after policy)

### 7. Findings
Fiscal policy helped reduce cost but benefits reached late to small factories.

### 8. Suggestions
GST should be kept at 5% for textiles and PLI scheme should include small units also.

### 9. Conclusion
Fiscal policy is very important for survival of traditional industries like Tiruppur textiles.
